# Dr. Of Comics: bugs and ideas

This is where you can report a bug or suggest a feature for **[Dr. Of Comics](https://drofcomics.com)**, the free comic collection app.

## How to post

1. Click **[New issue](https://github.com/alonatau/drofcomics-feedback/issues/new/choose)**.
2. Pick one:
   - **Bug report**: something is broken or looks wrong.
   - **Feature request**: an idea to make the app better.
   - **Comic not found**: a barcode or ISBN the scanner couldn't identify.
3. Fill in the form and click **Submit**.

You need a free GitHub account to post. No account? Use the [contact form](https://drofcomics.com/Contact) instead.

## Before you post

- Search the [open issues](https://github.com/alonatau/drofcomics-feedback/issues) first. If yours is there, add a 👍 instead of a new post.
- Everything here is **public**. Don't post your email, password, or other private details.

## What happens next

Every post gets read. Labels show where it stands: `planned`, `in progress`, `fixed`, or `needs info`.

Thanks for helping make Dr. Of Comics better! 🦸
